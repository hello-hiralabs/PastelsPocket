Common questions

How is my daily allowance calculated?

You split your monthly income into envelopes: bills, treats and savings. The treats envelope is spread across the days left in the month, so your allowance adjusts gently as you spend.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on sync. We have no servers and cannot see your data. See our privacy policy.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Turn on iCloud sync, or export a backup file from Settings and import it on your new phone. The backup is a file you keep; we never receive it.

I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?

Not necessarily. Streak Freezes cover missed days automatically, and the cat will simply be waiting for you. Pastel Pockets never punishes a busy week.

What are coins for?

You earn coins by closing your day. Spend them on new rooms for your cat in the Collection tab. Rooms you buy are yours for every cat, for good.

Can I share without showing my numbers?

Yes. Turn on "Hide my numbers" on the story card and your amounts are replaced by your cat's mood.

Is this financial advice?

No. Pastel Pockets is a journal to help you notice your spending. It does not give investment or financial advice.

Pertanyaan umum

Jatah jajan harian dihitung dari mana?

Pemasukan bulananmu dibagi ke amplop: tagihan, jajan, dan tabungan. Amplop jajan dibagi rata ke sisa hari di bulan itu, jadi jatahmu menyesuaikan pelan-pelan saat kamu belanja.

Dataku disimpan di mana?

Hanya di iPhone kamu, dan di iCloud pribadimu kalau sinkronisasi dinyalakan. Kami tidak punya server dan tidak bisa melihat datamu. Lihat kebijakan privasi.

Bagaimana cara backup atau pindah ke HP baru?

Nyalakan sinkronisasi iCloud, atau ekspor berkas backup dari Pengaturan lalu impor di HP barumu. Berkas itu milikmu; kami tidak pernah menerimanya.

Aku kelewat sehari. Streak-ku hilang?

Belum tentu. Libur Streak menutup hari yang terlewat secara otomatis, dan kucingmu cuma nungguin kamu balik. Pastel Pockets nggak pernah menghukum minggu yang sibuk.

Koin buat apa?

Koin didapat dari Tutup Buku tiap hari. Pakai untuk membeli kamar baru buat kucingmu di tab Koleksi. Kamar yang sudah dibeli jadi milikmu untuk semua kucing, selamanya.

Bisa berbagi tanpa menunjukkan nominal?

Bisa. Nyalakan "Sembunyikan nominal" di kartu story, dan nominalnya diganti suasana hati kucingmu.

Apakah ini saran keuangan?

Bukan. Pastel Pockets adalah jurnal untuk membantumu menyadari pengeluaran, bukan penasihat investasi atau keuangan.
